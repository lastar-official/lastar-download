BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know

When can I download?

The verified download will appear when applicable filing, identity, privacy and minimum safety requirements are met. No APK is available in the preparing state.

Will this update my existing app?

This beta uses com.lastar.greenfield.beta and coexists with the original OwnerReview app. Future beta updates require the same package and signing identity with a higher version. Do not uninstall or clear data.

How do I report a problem or request my data?

Email hello@lastar.me, handled by the individual operator. Do not send passwords, codes, identity documents or other people’s private content. Signing out does not delete the server account or message history.

App privacy notice status · App terms status

App-specific documents are not effective yet. Website privacy does not replace the App notice.
