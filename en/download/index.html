GOOD TO KNOW

A few things to know

Can I download it now?

Public download is in preparation. Authorized testers continue through their existing protected channel.

Will this update my existing app?

This channel uses com.lastar.greenfield.beta. Updates keep the same package and signature with a higher version. OwnerReview is a separate app and cannot update this beta.

How do I send feedback or make a data request?

Contact hello@lastar.me. Do not send passwords, codes, identity documents or other people’s private content. Signing out does not delete the server account or message history.
